The "Twist": Hoverslam

Unlike lunar landers, Earth has high gravity and an atmosphere. The Falcon 9 engine is too powerful to hover even at minimum throttle when the tank is empty.

  • Suicide Burn: You must time the burn to hit 0 velocity exactly at 0 altitude.
  • Top Heavy: The rocket is a tall cylinder, prone to tipping.
  • No Hovering: TWR > 1.0 means you always ascend or accelerate down unless perfectly timed.

Technical Aesthetic

Visuals focus on realism: Soot accumulation on the booster, underexpanded exhaust at high altitudes, and shock diamonds near the ground. Audio features sonic booms and cold gas thruster venting.

PHYSICS ENGINE

ENVIRONMENT: EARTH (9.8m/s²) | DRAG: VARIABLE

The physics model goes beyond simple arcade gravity. It simulates atmospheric drag, variable wind forces at altitude, and the critical relationship between fuel mass and thrust-to-weight ratio (TWR). Explore the simulation data below.

Thrust-to-Weight Ratio (TWR) INTERACTIVE

As fuel burns, the rocket becomes lighter. Adjust the fuel slider to see how the TWR changes. If TWR > 1.0 at Min Throttle, hovering is impossible.

The "Hoverslam" Corridor

The safe landing window is narrow. You must decelerate aggressively at the last second. Too early = run out of fuel. Too late = crash.

Gravity

Standard 9.8 m/s². Constant downward force requiring continuous management.

Atmosphere

Drag based on velocity. Wind applies lateral force, increasing with altitude.

Inertia

High moment of inertia (long cylinder). Rotation is slow; planning is required.

FLIGHT SYSTEMS

MECHANICS: GIMBAL | GRID FINS | LEGS

The Falcon 9 utilizes unique control mechanisms that differentiate it from standard lunar landers. Understanding these systems is key to survival.

Grid Fin Aerodynamics

Grid fins steer the rocket using airflow. Unlike RCS thrusters, they work better at high speeds.

Thrust Vectoring (Gimbal)

The entire engine bell pivots to steer during powered flight. The flame angles opposite to the turn direction.

Landing Legs

State Machine: Retracted → Deploying → Locked.

Warning: Deploying too early increases drag and risks structural failure. Deploying too late results in a crash.

Minimum Throttle

Engines have a "floor" of ~40%. You cannot throttle down to 0% without shutting off the engine (MECO).

MISSION PROGRESSION

CAMPAIGN MODE

The game scales difficulty by introducing altitude, wind, moving targets, and precision requirements.

Level 1: The Grasshopper

Tutorial Mission. Low altitude hop. No wind. Wide concrete pad.

DIFFICULTY: LOW

Level 2: Return to Launch Site (LZ-1)

Sub-orbital drop. Steer back to land. Wind introduced.

DIFFICULTY: MED

Level 3: "Of Course I Still Love You"

Ocean landing. Target is smaller and moving (bobbing in waves).

DIFFICULTY: HIGH

Level 4: Falcon Heavy Center Core

Higher entry speed. Land precisely between two side boosters (visual obstacles).

DIFFICULTY: EXTREME
